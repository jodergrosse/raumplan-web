What’s new

What changed in each version of RaumPlan. The latest version is at the top.

Version 1.1.0 December 2026

Plan

  • Shared walls: the wall between two rooms becomes one wall with thickness. Exterior walls in line are made flush.
  • Several floors sit at the right height on top of each other and aligned. Single walls at a stair opening no longer count towards the room height.
  • Stairs: shown on both floors, detected from the scan (also from your path up the stairs), editable points, and their own layer to hide them.
  • Sloped ceilings per room, also in the living area.
  • Areas and volumes per room and floor, plus materials and quantities (paint, flooring, tiles, skirting) with your own prices.
  • Notes and markers with photo and sketch.
  • Delete a room with everything in it, with a trash for 30 days.

Scan

  • Help in dark rooms, with flashlight.
  • Choose the floor while scanning, discard the room you just scanned.
  • Guided panorama capture and panoramas without double images, viewable all around on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

3D

  • Several floors together, stacked as a dollhouse on Vision Pro.
  • Photos on walls and floors, your own 3D furniture.
  • Daylight: sun position by location, date and time with shadows, a visible sun and compass directions, also on Vision Pro.
  • Point cloud with shading and highlighted edges, easy to read even without photos, adjustable point size.

Export

  • PDF floor plan, PDF report with photos and notes, image, room list (CSV), IFC, GLB, E57, LAS, 4K render and an HTML file with floor plan and 3D to share.
  • Share or save to Files directly on iPhone, iPad and Vision Pro.
  • Point cloud PLY files now have z up, like E57 and LAS. Older PLY files stay as they are.

Also

  • Projects from iCloud open faster: the plan is there right away, photos follow in the background.
  • Numbers everywhere in the format of your language.
  • Many improvements and fixes on Vision Pro.

Version 1.0 November 2026

First release for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro.

Included

  • Scan rooms with LiDAR, merge several rooms into one floor, coverage map and glass edge markers.
  • Plan editor with its own correction layer: walls, glass walls, doors, windows and objects, planned elements highlighted against what exists, correct lengths with a laser measurement.
  • 3D view, scanned mesh and point cloud.
  • On the Mac: compute the point cloud, floor image and panoramas from your photos.
  • Apple Vision Pro: dollhouse, tour through the panoramas and on-site view at 1:1 scale.
  • Export as SVG, DXF, JSON, PLY and OBJ.
  • Sync via iCloud Drive, no account and no data collected.
