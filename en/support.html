Support

Help with RaumPlan. The app also has a detailed guide: on iPhone, iPad and Vision Pro behind the question mark button, on the Mac under Help › RaumPlan Help (⌘?).

The app is shown in English unless your device is set to German, so button names here follow the English version.

Sample project

To try RaumPlan without a scan of your own, for example on the Mac or on Apple Vision Pro: a sample flat with six rooms and a planned walk-in closet.

Download sample project (zip, 3 KB)

Unzip the file and put the folder “Sample Flat.raumplan” into the RaumPlan folder in iCloud Drive. It then shows up in the project list. Or open it in the app with the folder button.

Frequently asked questions

“Scan” is greyed out

Your device has no LiDAR sensor. Scanning works on iPhone Pro (iPhone 12 Pro and later) and iPad Pro with LiDAR. Viewing and editing plans works on all supported devices.

How do I get a good scan?

Walk slowly along the walls, 1 to 3 m away, holding the device at chest height. Aim deliberately at corners, door reveals and windows, then sweep once over ceiling and floor. Don't leave the app between rooms so that all rooms line up. Scan rooms larger than about 9 × 9 m in two or three zones.

Tracking gets lost

Move more slowly, add light, and avoid looking at blank white walls or glass up close. Standing still for a moment usually helps.

A wall is missing or crooked

Draw it in the plan or drag the wall end. LiDAR often misses glass walls: while scanning, mark both ends with “Glass Edge” and tap “Glass Wall from Markers” in the plan.

How accurate are the measurements?

Usually within a few centimeters, though drift can build up over a whole floor. “Back at Start” shows the loop closure error. Measure important lengths with a laser and enter them under “Laser measurement” on the wall.

With photos or LiDAR only?

Photos are needed for panoramas, the Vision Pro tour, the floor image and the colored point cloud. Without photos you get the plan, mesh and a grey point cloud. Photos can be deleted later but not added afterwards.

How do I remove photos from a project?

Open the project, go to the “Project” tab (Vision Pro: bottom of the sidebar) and tap “Delete Photos from Project” under “Capture Data”. Plan, measurements, depth and mesh stay. This cannot be undone and applies to all devices for iCloud projects.

The project doesn't show up on my other device

Check that both devices are signed in with the same Apple Account and iCloud Drive is on, then refresh the project list. iCloud can take a moment. Without iCloud: share the project folder via AirDrop and load it with “Open Folder”.

Why is the project “read only”?

A project is edited on one device at a time. If it's open elsewhere, you see that device's changes within seconds. Tap “Take over” to move editing to your device.

The tour shows no image

That viewpoint has no panorama. On the Mac, recompute panoramas in the “Project” tab. If you scanned with “LiDAR Only” or deleted the photos, there is no panorama.

Which formats can I export?

SVG (1:50 or 1:100), DXF, JSON, the point cloud as PLY and the mesh as OBJ. Files are saved in the project folder under “exports”. There is no E57 or IFC export, but PLY can be converted, for example with CloudCompare.

Does RaumPlan cost anything?

No. RaumPlan is free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no account.
