Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026. The German version is legally binding; this is a translation.

In short: the RaumPlan app does not collect any personal data. Everything you scan and plan stays on your device or in your own iCloud storage. As the developer, I never see any of it.

1. Controller

Johannes Groß
Email: kontakt@raumplan-app.de

2. The RaumPlan app

No collection by the developer

RaumPlan has no user account, no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and no developer servers. The app does not send any data to me or to third parties. The App Store privacy label therefore says “Data Not Collected”.

What the app stores on your device

Each project is a folder on your device containing:

  • Plan: rooms, walls, doors, windows, objects and your corrections.
  • LiDAR data: depth images, surface mesh, camera positions and an environment map for continuing a scan later.
  • Compass direction: while scanning, the app uses the compass to roughly measure where north is and saves that direction in the project so the plan can be oriented to north. No location is determined and no location permission is requested.
  • Photos: camera images and panoramas, only if you scan in “With Photos” mode. In “LiDAR Only” mode no camera image is saved.

Photos taken in homes can show personal things such as people, documents or furnishings. You can remove them at any time in the app under Project › Capture Data › Delete Photos from Project. You delete a whole project in the project list. Deleting the app also deletes locally stored projects; projects in iCloud Drive remain there until you delete them.

Permissions

  • Camera (iPhone, iPad): Apple's room detection needs the camera and LiDAR sensor to recognize walls and furniture. Images are processed only on your device.
  • World sensing (Apple Vision Pro): needed to align the plan with the real room. This data is processed on the device only and is not stored.

iCloud Drive

If you use iCloud Drive, RaumPlan stores projects in your iCloud storage so they appear on your other devices. Apple handles this transfer and storage as part of your iCloud account, under Apple's Privacy Policy. I have no access to your iCloud storage. To stop two devices from editing at the same time, the app saves a small file in the project folder with the name of the device currently editing (e.g. “John's iPhone”); it stays in your project.

Sharing and export

When you share a project, photo or export (for example via AirDrop, Mail or the Files app), you do so through operating-system features at your own initiative. What the recipient does with it is outside the app.

App Store and crash reports

Apple processes data under its own responsibility for downloads from the App Store. If you agreed in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple provides me with aggregated usage figures and anonymized crash reports that cannot be traced back to you. You can turn this off at any time under Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.
